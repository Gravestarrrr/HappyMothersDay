Happy Mother's Day!

I love you, Mom!

Dear Mom, I cannot express how much I miss you. Life has been a rollercoaster of emotions for the both of us, but I'm grateful God paired us two together. You're the best mother a son could ever have. Thank you for all of your constant sacrifices. It hurts everyday that I cannot give you the world as I want to right away, but you have taught me patience and how things truly do come with time. I may not have a lot of to give, but I hope this creative form of a card can show you what I have been up to without having to explain over the phone or text. You are the reason I am the person I am today, so thank you for being you. I know you're being very strong for Angelita and I am confident she acknowledges all of your stress and love. I wish I could be with you everyday and just leave the whole human act we do for society. Sometimes it really is hard to get out of bead and keep showing up. I wish things go back to the good ole days where we would spend a lot of time together and I didn't even realize those times will pass so quickly. I am proud of you and all the hard work you have put into life. I may not say it or show it, but writing it all out helps me express my emotions in ways I couldn't say in person. Thank you for putting up with me, for having me, and for living such an amazing life. You're the strongest person I know, me and you forever and always. I love you to the moon and back always and forever. Please live forever.

Mama's World
